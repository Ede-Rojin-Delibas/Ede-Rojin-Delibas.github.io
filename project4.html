E-Commerce Exploratory Data Analysis

Uncovering customer behavior, revenue drivers, and operational insights from transaction data

1. Introduction

In today's highly competitive e-commerce landscape, understanding customer behavior, revenue drivers, and operational efficiency is crucial for sustainable growth.

This project explores a real-world e-commerce dataset to uncover key patterns in customer purchasing behavior, product performance, and delivery operations. Through comprehensive exploratory data analysis (EDA), the goal is to transform raw transactional data into actionable business insights.

2. Problem Statement

E-commerce businesses often struggle with low customer retention, inefficient logistics, and unclear revenue drivers.

Key questions addressed in this project:

  • What drives revenue in the business?
  • Why is customer retention low?
  • How does delivery performance affect customer satisfaction?
  • Are there seasonal trends affecting sales performance?

3. Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

  • Analyze customer purchasing behavior
  • Identify key revenue drivers
  • Evaluate delivery performance and its impact on satisfaction
  • Detect seasonal trends and anomalies
  • Provide actionable business recommendations

4. Data Sources

The dataset consists of 112,650 transaction-level records and 36 features, covering the period between 2016 and 2018.

Each row represents an individual order item, including:

  • Product details
  • Customer information
  • Payment data
  • Delivery timestamps
  • Review scores

Additional features were engineered to support time-based and behavioral analysis.

5. Methodology

5.1 Data Preparation

  • Merged multiple datasets into a unified structure
  • Converted data types (timestamps, categorical features)
  • Handled missing values and outliers
  • Engineered new features:
    • Revenue
    • Delivery time
    • Time-based features (month, weekday)
    • Delivery segments

5.2 Univariate Analysis

  • Analyzed distributions of price, revenue, freight cost
  • Identified right-skewed distributions
  • Detected significant outliers

Revenue Distribution: The histogram shows a right-skewed distribution with most transactions clustered at lower values, indicating a long tail of high-value orders.

5.3 Bivariate Analysis

Examined relationships between:

  • Price vs Payment Value
  • Delivery Time vs Review Score
  • Freight vs Weight

Price Patterns: Similar right-skewed distribution observed, with most products priced in the lower range.

5.4 Multivariate Analysis

Correlation heatmap used to analyze relationships across multiple variables. Identified strong relationships between Price & Payment Value and Weight & Freight Cost.

Correlations: The heatmap reveals strong positive correlations between product characteristics and their pricing/cost metrics.

5.5 Segment Analysis

Segmented data by:

  • Product categories
  • Delivery speed
  • Payment types

Category Performance: Certain categories consistently outperform others in terms of order volume.

5.6 Time-Based Analysis

Monthly revenue trends analyzed, weekly purchase patterns examined, and seasonal effects and anomalies identified.

Revenue Trends: Clear seasonal patterns with a notable anomaly in September showing a sharp decline.

6. Evaluation & Results

The analysis revealed several important patterns:

  • Revenue distribution is highly skewed, with a small number of high-value transactions contributing significantly
  • Most customers make only a single purchase, indicating low retention
  • Delivery time is generally slow, with potential impact on customer satisfaction
  • Strong seasonality patterns exist, with a sharp drop observed in September

Log Transformation: Applying log transformation to revenue helps normalize the heavily skewed distribution for better statistical analysis.

Delivery Performance: Most deliveries take longer than optimal, suggesting logistics optimization opportunities.

7. Key Business Insights & Business Value

7.1 Revenue Insight

Revenue is primarily driven by a small number of high-value transactions.

Business Value: Increase average order value | Focus on premium customers

7.2 Customer Insight

Customer retention is low, with most users making only one purchase.

Business Value: Loyalty programs needed | Reduce acquisition cost

7.3 Logistics Insight

Delivery delays negatively impact customer satisfaction.

Business Value: Optimize logistics | Improve customer experience

7.4 Category Insight

A small number of categories dominate total revenue.

Business Value: Focus inventory & marketing

7.5 Trend Insight

Strong seasonality exists with a critical anomaly in September.

Business Value: Optimize campaigns | Investigate operational issues
